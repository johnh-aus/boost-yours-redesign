About Jay — BetterLife Property Group

Jay is a real estate sales agent at BetterLife Property Group. Like most agents, his pipeline depended on referrals and personal networking — effective, but unpredictable and slow to scale. He partnered with Boost Yours to build a steady, reliable flow of qualified buyer and seller enquiries he could convert into sales.

The Problem

A Great Agent.
An Unpredictable Pipeline.

For an individual real estate agent, the hardest part isn't closing — it's a consistent flow of people to talk to. Relying on referrals and cold networking means feast-or-famine months, with no control over how many opportunities land in any given week.

Jay needed a predictable source of qualified leads — real prospects ready to have a conversation — and a system to turn those leads into consultations and signed deals, instead of hoping the phone would ring.

The brief: "Fill my calendar with qualified prospects and turn them into sales."

Our approach

The Agent Lead Generation System

1

Attract

We launched targeted lead-generation campaigns built around Jay's market and ideal client — putting his name and offer in front of active buyers and sellers. In the first month alone the system generated 40+ qualified leads.

2

Convert

Leads only matter if they turn into conversations. We built a follow-up and booking flow that moved enquiries into real appointments — converting the new pipeline into 10+ consultations in the same month.

3

Close

With a full calendar of qualified prospects, Jay could do what he does best. The result in month one: 4 closed sales — a fast, tangible return that proved the system works from day one.
