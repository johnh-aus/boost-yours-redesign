About Billbergia

Billbergia is one of Sydney's leading property developers, delivering large-scale residential and mixed-use projects across prime Sydney locations. With premium developments and a discerning buyer audience, the challenge was generating volume without sacrificing lead quality.

The Problem

New Development.
Zero Market Awareness.

Billbergia needed to generate serious buyer interest for a new property development — fast. Traditional developer marketing was slow, expensive, and poorly targeted. There was a short window to create momentum and fill the pipeline before launch momentum faded.

The real challenge was qualifying high-ticket buyers efficiently. Not everyone who clicks an ad is ready to commit to a premium property. They needed a system that attracted the right people, filtered out time-wasters, and moved serious buyers toward a consultation — at scale.

The brief: "Generate qualified buyer enquiries quickly, and make every dollar of ad spend count."

Our approach

The Acquisition System

1

Attract

Premium creative campaigns built specifically for high-net-worth buyer segments. The messaging positioned the development as a limited, exclusive opportunity — driving urgency and serious interest from the start.

2

Convert

A dedicated landing page and qualification system filtered enquiries before they reached the sales team. Only buyers who met pre-set criteria were routed through to a consultation booking — saving time and increasing close rates.

3

Scale

Retargeting sequences kept warm prospects engaged throughout the consideration phase. As one project launched, the pipeline structure was designed to scale across future developments without rebuilding from scratch.
