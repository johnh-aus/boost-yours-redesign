My Korean School
(MKS Melbourne)

How a language school doubled enrolment from 160 to 300+ students in just 6 months — using targeted Meta ads, automated follow-up sequences, and a rebuilt booking funnel.

Industry
Language Education
Timeframe
6 Months
System Used
Meta Ads + CRM Automation
Outcome
160 → 300+ Students

The Problem

A Great School With
An Invisible Presence.

MKS Melbourne had been teaching Korean language to adults and children for years — with consistently glowing reviews and strong word-of-mouth. But their enrolments had plateaued at around 160 students, and they had no reliable digital marketing system to grow beyond their existing network.

Their social media was inconsistent. Their website generated almost no enquiries. And every time they ran a promotion, the results were unpredictable. They were at a classic growth ceiling: great product, invisible business.

When they came to Boost Yours, the brief was simple: "Help us fill our classes and keep them full."

Our approach

The 3-Step Growth System

We don't run random campaigns. We build interconnected systems where each step feeds the next.

1

Attract

We rebuilt MKS Melbourne's Meta ad strategy from scratch — targeting parents of school-age children and Korean culture enthusiasts in Melbourne with video testimonial ads and before/after enrolment stories. We also added Google Search ads targeting high-intent keywords like "Korean class Melbourne."

2

Convert

We replaced their generic homepage with a dedicated landing page designed for one action: book a trial class. The page featured social proof, clear course options, and a frictionless booking form. Conversion rate went from 2% to 11% in week one.

3

Retain

We built an automated email and SMS sequence that nurtured new trial bookings into paid enrolments — and re-engaged past students at the start of each term. This alone recovered an estimated 40 students who had previously dropped off the radar.
