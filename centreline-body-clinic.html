About Centreline Body Clinic

Centreline Body Clinic is a Sydney Pilates studio offering one-to-one and small-group tower reformer sessions focused on posture and pain relief — for clients from office workers to seniors and golfers.

The Challenge

Loyal Clients.
Tied to One Person.

Clients came to Centreline because of the founder, not the brand — which made growth hard to scale. Organic content alone wasn't turning followers into first bookings.

What we did

From Founder to Brand

1

Story

Built the Centreline brand story and testimonial content so the studio itself — not only its founder — earned trust.

2

Trial

Meta campaigns on Facebook and Instagram offering a first-session trial, with DM enquiries and landing-page bookings.

3

Retarget

Retargeting warm audiences who had engaged with the studio but not yet booked.
