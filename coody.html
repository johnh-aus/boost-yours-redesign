About Coody

Coody is an Australian brand selling premium inflatable “air tents” that go up without poles, alongside air mattresses, an air sofa and camping accessories, with free shipping Australia-wide. Its customers are families and campers who want a faster, sturdier setup.

The Challenge

A Premium Product.
No Sales Data Yet.

When we started in June 2026, Coody's ads were getting clicks but no purchases yet — not enough conversion data for Meta's algorithm to learn from, heading into the camping low season.

Early videos were long and viewers dropped off within seconds. Coody also competes with cheaper look-alike products, and its tents are a high-ticket purchase — shoppers needed a clear, fast reason to choose the original.

What we did

Meta + Google Search Growth

1

Hook

Short-form creative built around two angles: “Still fighting poles?” — set up in under five minutes with no poles — and a wind test showing the tent standing firm where a regular tent fails.

2

Focus

A Meta sales campaign targeting families in Sydney, Melbourne and Brisbane, with budget concentrated on the best-performing videos. A separate traffic campaign supported Coody at a Melbourne expo.

3

Capture

Google Search ads added in August 2026 to reach people already searching for air tents and the Coody brand.
