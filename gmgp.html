About Good Meat Good People

Good Meat Good People (GMGP) is a Sydney butcher and online retailer of premium Korean-BBQ cuts — Wagyu, Angus, pork, marinades and hotpot — serving home customers and restaurants, with chilled home delivery and stores across Sydney.

The Challenge

Great Product.
No Time to Run Ads.

GMGP was growing on two fronts at once — home delivery and new stores. The in-house team had no capacity to plan, produce and optimise advertising alongside daily operations, so online growth depended on whatever time was left over.

What we did

Seasonal Sales System

1

Deliver

Home-delivery sales campaigns with separate ads for new customers and for retargeting past visitors and buyers.

2

Season

Package and seasonal campaigns built around Christmas and events, with agency-produced video ads and posters.

3

Launch

Store-opening campaigns to drive local awareness and visits to new locations.
