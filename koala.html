About Koala

Koala is an Australian furniture and homewares brand known for its premium mattresses and lifestyle products. Looking to expand into the Korean market, Koala needed a strategy that would resonate with a new audience — and deliver measurable results quickly.

The Problem

A Strong Brand.
An Untapped Market.

Koala had an established presence and strong brand equity — but the Korean market represented a significant growth opportunity that hadn't been fully activated. Entering a new market at scale requires more than translating an existing strategy: local consumer behaviour, cultural positioning, and market-specific acquisition channels all need to be purpose-built.

The challenge was executing a market expansion quickly and efficiently — without disrupting the core business — while identifying the right product positioning and channel mix for Korean consumers specifically.

The brief: "Build a playbook that works in Korea. Make the numbers move fast."

Our approach

The Market Expansion System

1

Attract

A market-entry strategy was built around deep understanding of Korean consumer behaviour and purchasing patterns. Acquisition campaigns were designed to match the channels and content formats that resonated locally — not a direct translation of the existing approach, but a purpose-built Korean growth system.

2

Convert

Product positioning was refined for the Korean market, emphasising the attributes and values that drove purchase decisions locally. The conversion pathway was optimised for the specific behaviours and expectations of Korean online shoppers, reducing drop-off and improving purchase rates meaningfully.

3

Scale

Once the core acquisition system was validated and revenue momentum was established, a full product group launch was planned and executed within month five. The expansion created a new revenue pillar and established Koala as a scalable multi-market operation.
