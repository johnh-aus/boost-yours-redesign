About My Korean School

My Korean School is a Melbourne-based Korean language school offering structured programs for adults and young learners. With a passionate teaching team and strong student outcomes, the school had built a loyal following through word-of-mouth — but growth had hit a ceiling.

The Problem

A Great School.
Not Enough Students.

My Korean School had an excellent programme and committed students — but growth had hit a ceiling at around 160 enrolments. The school was primarily reaching new students through word-of-mouth and community referrals, which couldn't reliably scale enrolment numbers past a certain point.

The challenge was building a predictable, repeatable student acquisition system. Without one, enrolment growth was seasonal, inconsistent, and heavily dependent on existing students' networks. Great teaching alone doesn't fill classrooms — a system does.

The brief: "Double the student count. Build something that keeps working."

Our approach

The Enrolment Growth System

1

Attract

Targeted acquisition campaigns were built to reach people actively looking to learn Korean — students, K-drama and K-pop enthusiasts, professionals, and heritage language learners. Each audience segment was reached with messaging tailored to their specific motivation for learning.

2

Convert

A structured enrolment funnel was built to convert interested prospects into paying students efficiently. Automated follow-up sequences handled enquiries at scale, ensuring no lead fell through the cracks. The booking and trial class experience was designed to maximise conversion from first contact to enrolment.

3

Scale

With the acquisition funnel running consistently, the focus shifted to increasing enrolment velocity and reducing churn. Retargeting campaigns re-engaged warm prospects. The 11% conversion rate from enquiry to enrolment created a reliable, scalable growth engine that operated independently of word-of-mouth.
