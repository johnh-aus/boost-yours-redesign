About OZEKI Group

OZEKI Group is an established Japanese food court franchise with 8 years of trading history and 8 branches across Sydney and Newcastle. Despite a long track record and a loyal local following, the brand had little visibility online and a reputation that didn't reflect the quality of the food — leaving growth on the table at every location.

The Problem

Great Food.
A Reputation That Didn't Show It.

After eight years in business, OZEKI Group had built a solid operation — but online, the brand was nearly invisible. Across its branches, review scores sat in the 3.0–3.5★ range, well below what the food and service deserved, and far below what customers expect when choosing where to eat.

In a food court, the decision is made in seconds. A weak star rating and a thin review count send customers straight to the stall next door. With no consistent brand, no review engine, and an outdated digital presence, OZEKI was losing diners — and high-value catering enquiries — before they ever walked up.

The brief: "Make the reputation match the food — and turn it into sales across all 8 branches."

Our approach

The Reputation & Brand System

1

Rebuild

We refreshed the OZEKI brand and built a new website designed to convert — clean, fast, and built around the menu, the locations, and a clear path to order and enquire. The brand finally looked as good as the food, creating one consistent identity across all 8 branches.

2

Reputation

We installed a review-generation engine across every location, making it effortless for happy customers to leave a review at the right moment. Within months OZEKI had gathered 1,000+ new reviews and lifted its rating from the 3.0–3.5★ range to a trusted 4.5★+.

3

Grow

With a strong rating and stronger brand awareness in place, we turned attention into revenue — driving local discovery and opening up a dedicated catering channel that brought in higher-value orders beyond the everyday food court foot traffic.
