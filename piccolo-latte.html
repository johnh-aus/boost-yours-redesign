About Piccolo Latte

Piccolo Latte is a specialty coffee café that opened as a brand-new store with no existing customers, no identity, and no awareness in its local market. The owners had a clear vision for the coffee — what they needed was a brand and a launch that would make the whole town notice from day one.

The Problem

A Brand-New Store.
Nobody Knew It Existed.

Opening a new café is the hardest moment in hospitality. There's no reputation, no regulars, and no word-of-mouth — just rent, fit-out costs, and a quiet store. Most new cafés spend their first months bleeding cash while they wait to be discovered.

Piccolo Latte needed more than a soft opening. It needed a complete brand — name, logo, design and identity — and a launch loud enough to fill the store immediately, before the early-stage costs ate into the business.

The brief: "Open with a bang. Make Piccolo Latte the café everyone in town is talking about."

Our approach

The Brand Launch & Awareness System

1

Brand

We built Piccolo Latte from the ground up — logo, design, and a complete brand identity that gave the new store an instantly recognisable, premium feel. Every touchpoint was designed to look established from the very first day.

2

Launch

We ran a high-impact local awareness campaign timed to the opening. Within two weeks, the campaign had people physically lined up out the door — turning a brand-new store into the most talked-about café in town and the recognised #1 locally.

3

Scale

With awareness established, we launched a second campaign focused on conversion and repeat visits. The result: a 200% revenue increase in a single month — momentum that turned a fresh opening into a thriving local favourite.
