Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. Introduction

Boost Yours ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information.

This policy explains how we collect, use, and store information when you visit our website, engage with our services, or communicate with us.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following information:

  • Personal details (name, email address, phone number, company name) submitted through forms or chats.
  • Business and marketing information you share for consultation or quotation purposes.
  • Technical data such as IP address, browser type, cookies, and analytics tracking (via Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, etc).

3. How We Use Your Information

Your data is used to:

  • Respond to inquiries, provide quotes, and deliver marketing services.
  • Improve our website, advertising, and customer experience.
  • Send relevant updates, offers, or marketing materials (you may opt out anytime).
  • Maintain legal, accounting, and business records.

We do not sell or rent your information to third parties.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is securely stored using encrypted cloud platforms (Google Workspace, GoHighLevel, and related tools). Access is restricted to authorised team members only.

5. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party tools that process limited personal data on our behalf, such as:

  • Meta (Facebook/Instagram) for advertising
  • Google Ads and Analytics
  • GoHighLevel CRM
  • Mailgun for email delivery

These providers may store information outside Australia but comply with standard international data protection laws.

6. Cookies

Our website uses cookies and pixels to track activity, improve ads, and analyse visitor behaviour. You can disable cookies through your browser, but some site features may not function correctly.

7. Your Rights

You may request:

  • Access to personal data we hold
  • Correction or deletion of inaccurate data
  • Removal from our marketing communications

To do so, contact us at info@boost-yours.com.

8. Policy Updates

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational or legal changes. The latest version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this policy, contact:

  • Sydney, NSW, Australia
  • Email: info@boost-yours.com
