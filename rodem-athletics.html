About Rodem Athletics

Rodem Athletics is a Sydney performance and strength coaching business, offering Korean-speaking one-to-one personal training alongside team physical training for football and fencing.

The Challenge

Real Expertise.
Hard to See From Outside.

The head coach's expertise was the business — but it was hard to communicate. Serious, technical training content struggled to hold attention, and as a largely one-person operation there was little time left for marketing.

The task: make the coach's expertise and the value of the training visible to the right clients, and turn that into demand.

What we did

Expert Personal Branding

1

Position

Built the brand around the head coach's expertise and differentiated approach to performance and strength training.

2

Simplify

Turned technical training knowledge into simple, visually hooked content that people actually stop to watch.

3

Reach

Meta campaigns on Instagram reaching Korean-speaking audiences in Sydney, with retargeting and DM-based enquiries.

The results

Results in 6 Months

0×
Revenue in 6 Months
1:1 → Group
Services Expanded
Solo → Team
Organisation Growth

Expanded services: beyond one-to-one personal training into group training for football and fencing teams. A growing team: from a one-person operation to team-based coaching.

For scale: the head coach currently runs around 38–42 hours of one-to-one PT a week. Revenue growth measured over six months, as reported by Rodem Athletics.
