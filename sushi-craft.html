About Sushi Craft

Sushi Craft is a sushi takeaway franchise operating four branches. With a proven product and multiple locations already trading, the business partnered with Boost Yours to bring consistent local awareness and a structured approach to revenue growth across the whole group — replacing ad-hoc marketing with one repeatable system.

The Problem

Multiple Branches.
No Shared Growth Engine.

A multi-branch franchise has a unique challenge: each location competes in its own local market, yet they all share one brand. Without a coordinated system, awareness is uneven — some branches thrive while others stay quiet, and revenue is unpredictable across the group.

Sushi Craft had the product and the footprint, but needed consistent local visibility at every branch and a measurable path to revenue targets — not guesswork that has to be rebuilt for each new location.

The brief: "Make every branch known in its neighbourhood, and make growth repeatable across all four."

Our approach

The Local Awareness & Revenue System

1

Onboard

We onboarded all four branches into one growth system — auditing each location, aligning the brand, and setting clear revenue targets per branch. One structure, applied consistently, so every location works from the same playbook.

2

Awareness

We're driving local awareness branch by branch — putting Sushi Craft in front of the right neighbourhood audiences and turning each location into a recognised local choice for sushi takeaway, not just another option on the strip.

3

Grow

With awareness building, the focus is converting attention into orders and tracking revenue against target at every branch — an ongoing, measurable system designed to scale cleanly as the franchise expands.
