About Vision Asset Group

Vision Asset Group is an Australian property investment and development group specialising in off-the-plan residential projects. With a high-value product and a long sales cycle, the business needed a reliable pipeline of qualified buyer enquiries — not just traffic.

The Problem

Referral Dependent.
No Scalable Lead System.

Vision Asset Group had a strong track record in off-the-plan property sales, but their entire business was built on referrals. When the referrals slowed, so did the pipeline — and there was no systematic way to replace that lost volume.

Buyer acquisition was inconsistent. Follow-up was entirely manual. The team couldn't handle pipeline growth without a proper structure, and without scalable lead flow, ambitious revenue targets remained out of reach.

They came to Boost Yours needing a predictable, repeatable system — not just more leads, but a full acquisition engine that could grow with the team.

Our Approach

The 3-Step Growth System

We don't run random campaigns. We build interconnected systems where each step feeds the next.

1

Attract

Paid lead campaigns and seminar funnels targeting off-the-plan property buyers across Melbourne. Audience segmentation to reach investors and first-home buyers actively researching property purchases.

2

Convert

Consultation booking system with automated follow-up sequences to move leads from enquiry to booked appointment. Reduced manual effort while increasing the speed and consistency of buyer engagement.

3

Scale

Retargeting campaigns and a structured buyer pipeline enabled sustained lead volume. The system supported team growth from 7 to 14 staff — the business had the infrastructure to match its ambitions.
